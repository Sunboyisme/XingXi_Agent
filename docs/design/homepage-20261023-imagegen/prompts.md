## 01-cream-space · 奶油留白

预期图片：`01-cream-space.png`

最终提示词：

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop application homepage design proposal for the existing Chinese personal AI assistant XiaoXi.
Render ONE finished, exceptionally polished app screenshot, flat front-on, landscape 16:10 composition, preferably 1920x1200 or similar high resolution. Entire app window visible and perfectly readable, filling the canvas; no desktop background, no laptop mockup, no perspective, no marketing presentation, no external annotations or design-board titles. Simplified Chinese UI text, correct crisp typography, normal Chinese letter spacing. Realistic implementable desktop UI with careful spacing, restrained visual hierarchy and fine line icons.
Product identity: XiaoXi is a personal assistant for conversation, projects, learning, schedules and saved knowledge. Established warm cream/apricot design language; mascot is a simple FLAT terracotta circular orb with exactly TWO vertical ivory pill eyes, no mouth, no limbs, no glossy 3D. Use this mark consistently, inspired by supplied local icon.
Common shell in every proposal: a slim native Windows title bar with collapse icon at top-left, unobtrusive minimize/maximize/close at top-right. A narrow 68px far-left navigation rail containing small mascot at top and stacked outline icons for conversation, learning and calendar, gear at bottom. Beside rail, a 228px conversation sidebar with XiaoXi wordmark, a peach rounded "+ 新聊天" button, search icon, three left-aligned expandable groups "置顶", "项目", "对话" with small understated counts and right aligned plus/more icons. Only a few realistic example rows such as "产品设计思考", "个人学习", "整理本周计划", "阅读笔记". Sidebar is quiet and secondary, not crowded.
Mandatory composer: one prominent COMPACT two-line frosted-glass input surface, warm semitransparent tint, fine border and moderate rounded corners. Upper text field exact placeholder "输入消息…". Bottom toolbar: circular "+" at left (attachments, MCP and skills are inside this menu, NOT separate buttons); a small "思考" control and subtle model selector "当前模型" near right, peach circular upward-arrow send button far-right. No permanently exposed attachment/MCP/skill buttons. No huge text field. No thick nested borders. Project selector "未加入项目" may sit just above composer. Small notification bell top-right of main workspace.
Keep all dates consistent: "10月23日 周五". Homepage mock data must be generic, not personal. No invented account avatar or billing widgets, no analytics dashboards, no unrelated weather widget. No watermark. Do not draw the reference app icon as a giant square; use its circular face in the UI.

Primary request: Proposal 01 — quiet cream whitespace with conversation as the unmistakable center.
Style: warm near-white workspace #fffcf8, softly contrasting cream sidebar #f4ece3, terracotta text highlights, dark cocoa typography. Spacious, mature, calm, clean product craft.
Layout: center a small flat mascot directly above main greeting "今天想做点什么？". Center the hero and a wide compact composer in upper-middle of main pane, with generous side whitespace. Under composer show five small outline suggestion pills "安排今天", "整理文字", "解释概念", "提炼重点", "随便聊聊". Beneath this, two understated open sections aligned to composer width: "今日安排" with two time rows "14:00  阅读与笔记" and "16:30  整理本周计划"; "继续上次" with "个人学习" and "产品设计思考". Use hairline separators rather than card containers for lower sections. Main greeting is large but not giant. Lots of breathing room and an unmistakable input focal point. No decorative illustrations beyond mascot.
```
